# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect privacy and remove personal data from logs and examples.
- Respect copyright, licenses, clean-room boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, the game process, or the
  legal health of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
credential sharing, publishing private correspondence, copyright infringement,
and pressuring contributors to use leaked or proprietary material are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an available private
GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation in proportion
to the behavior and its impact. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
